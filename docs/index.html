Made for listening

A capable music player for Android

Find tracks, shape playback, follow lyrics, and keep your library close. Feature availability can depend on your device, region, selected provider, and account.

♫

Music discovery

Search and browse songs, artists, albums, playlists, charts, and radio stations from supported sources.

“

Synchronized lyrics

Find lyrics across configurable providers, follow timed lines, translate supported lyrics, and share lyric cards.

◖

Audio controls

Adjust the equalizer and supported effects, including low-pass filtering, spatial modes, normalization, and more.

⇩

Offline listening

Download available streams, cache music, manage storage, and configure download quality and network behavior.

⤴

Player your way

Customize the player, artwork colors, widget, queue, output-device view, and optional floating playback surfaces.

◉

Library and integrations

Keep favorites and listening history, make encrypted backups, and optionally connect Last.fm, Discord, and iShare.

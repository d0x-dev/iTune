Quick Installation Walkthrough

iTune is distributed directly without gatekeepers or store telemetry. Follow these 3 simple steps to get started on your Android device:

1

Download Package

Tap the APK download button below. For most phones, the universal package delivers instant, complete compatibility.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

When Android displays the system prompt, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser in Settings to proceed.

3

Launch & Play

Open iTune, allow local media storage access, and experience synchronized lyrics with zero telemetry or ads.

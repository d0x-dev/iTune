# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that supports a positive environment include:

- Being considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Welcoming differing viewpoints and experience.
- Giving and accepting feedback with care.
- Focusing discussion on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or other conduct that makes participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-maintained channels. It also applies when someone is representing the project in a public space.

Report an incident privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not post private or identifying details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible. The project owner may remove content, close or restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the incident. Maintainers are expected to communicate the reason for enforcement when doing so is safe and practical.

# Accessibility statement

iTune aims to make music discovery, playback, and library management usable by as many people as possible. Accessibility is an ongoing part of development, and the project welcomes reports that identify barriers.

## Current approach

The project aims to:
- Use clear labels and meaningful descriptions for interactive controls.
- Preserve readable text and sufficient contrast across supported themes.
- Support Android accessibility services, including TalkBack, where the underlying screen and component support it.
- Make touch targets and navigation practical on supported device sizes.
- Avoid relying on colour alone to communicate important state.

These are development goals, not a claim that every screen currently meets a particular accessibility standard. Some custom visualisations, animations, third-party content, and provider-controlled screens may have limitations.

## Report an accessibility barrier

If you encounter an accessibility problem, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/d0x-dev/iTune/issues) using the bug report template. Include:
- The screen or feature where the issue occurs.
- The Android version and device model.
- The assistive technology and relevant settings used (for example, TalkBack, font size, or display size).
- Steps to reproduce the barrier and what you expected to happen.

Please avoid sharing personal or sensitive information in screenshots and logs. You do not need to disclose a disability or diagnosis. For security vulnerabilities, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

## Ongoing improvements

Accessibility feedback is reviewed alongside other project issues. Improvements may be limited by Android platform behavior, device vendors, third-party services, or the available maintainers and testing devices.
